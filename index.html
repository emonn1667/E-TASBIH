What Makes MyTasbih Different

A tasbih counter is a tool for counting dhikr, the remembrance of Allah. MyTasbih is a free digital version with features built for real daily practice.

🧘

Focus Mode

Dims the screen and removes everything except your counter. Just you and your dhikr.

🕌

Sunnah Mode

Guides you through the 33-33-34 after-prayer dhikr automatically.

📊

Progress Tracking

See your daily count, lifetime total, and consecutive-day streak.

🎯

Custom Goals

Choose 33, 66, 99, unlimited, or set any custom target.

📴

Works Offline

Once loaded, the counter works without internet. Perfect for the mosque.

🌙

Dark Mode

Easier on the eyes during night-time dhikr and Tahajjud prayers.

💾

Auto-Save

Close the browser and come back anytime. Your progress is preserved.

📿

Custom Dhikr

Add your own dhikr phrases and track them alongside the built-in ones.

Morning & Evening Adhkar

Start and end your day with dhikr at your own pace.

🚶

During Commute or Travel
